बैंक के बारे में (About ICICI Bank)

ICICI Bank Limited is a leading Indian private sector commercial bank and is categorized by the Reserve Bank of India as a Domestic Systemically Important Bank (D-SIB). Renowned for pioneering modern digital banking infrastructure in India, ICICI Bank operates an expansive network of branches and ATMs, alongside digital platforms including iMobile Pay, InstaBIZ, and Trade Online.

नियामक स्थिति (Regulatory Status): Domestic Systemically Important Bank (D-SIB) / Scheduled Commercial Bank
